Harish-Chandra Collected Papers (Springer-Verlag Classification)

His research journey spans four distinct historical epochs, bridging quantum theoretical physics to pure representation theory:

Volume 1 (1944–1954)

33 Papers: Cosmic Ray Unit (IISc Bangalore), TIFR Bombay, Cambridge, Harvard, Columbia & IAS Princeton.

Volume 2 (1955–1958)

17 Papers: Columbia University & IAS Princeton. Breakthroughs on Plancherel formula for semisimple groups.

Volume 3 (1959–1968)

20 Papers: Joint works with Armand Borel, discrete series, and differential operators on reductive groups.

Volume 4 (1970–1983)

15 Papers: Princeton IAS. Pioneered $p$-adic harmonic analysis and supertempered distributions.

Then & Now: The Living Contemporary Nexus

Demonstrating how Harish-Chandra's abstract theorems directly run inside modern technology

Equivariant AI & Geometric Deep Learning

Then (1951–1954): Harish-Chandra introduced algebraic modules to preserve transformations under infinite-dimensional continuous groups.
Now (2020s–Present): Modern graph neural networks (AlphaFold 2, SE(3)-Transformers) use these exact representation theorems to guarantee rotational and reflectional symmetry in 3D molecular drug discovery.

The Langlands Program & Quantum Physics

Then (1958–1970): Formulated the $c$-function and discrete series representations, bridging differential geometry with non-compact groups.
Now (2020s–Present): Forms the indispensable foundation for Robert Langlands' grand unification of number theory, topological quantum field theory, and dualities in string physics.

Harmonic Analysis & Medical Imaging

Then (1960–1983): Developed the Harish-Chandra Schwartz space and transform to synthesize functions on curved symmetric Riemannian spaces.
Now (2020s–Present): Used in non-Euclidean Fourier transforms powering compressed sensing for MRI machines, radar array synthesis, and seismic wave exploration.
