Then & Now: The Contemporary Nexus

Bridging Harish-Chandra's mid-century formulas with 21st-century technological frontiers

Equivariant AI & Geometric Deep Learning

Then: Harish-Chandra classified infinite-dimensional representations of reductive Lie groups under continuous transformations.

Now: Modern neural networks (E(3)-equivariant GNNs) rely on representation theory for molecular docking, protein folding, and 3D computer vision.

Langlands Program & Quantum Physics

Then: Established explicit harmonic analysis on semisimple groups and character formulas alongside Armand Borel and P.A.M. Dirac.

Now: Serves as the bedrock of the Geometric Langlands correspondence, topological quantum field theory, and high-energy string dualities.

Harmonic Analysis & Signal Wavelets

Then: Formulated Harish-Chandra Schwartz Spaces and Plancherel measures for non-compact symmetric spaces.

Now: Used in generalized Fourier transforms for seismic imaging, medical tomography (CT/MRI), and advanced radar signal decomposition.
